# Foundations

Eleven lessons, one per chapter of Dayan & Abbott's *Theoretical Neuroscience*, plus a synthesis checkpoint. About 60 hours.

!!! note "Order"
    Lessons branch after F1 into two arms that later rejoin. F11 needs both F9 and F3.

    ```
    F1 ──┬── F2 ── F3 ── F4
         └── F5 ──┬── F6 ── F8 ── F9 ──┬── F10
                   └── F7                └── F11 (also needs F3)
    ```

!!! tip "Exercises"
    Every lesson assigns the textbook authors' own exercises for its chapter, available free from their site. Where the course's own exercises overlap, the authors' set takes precedence. Chapter 1's set includes twenty minutes of real recordings from a fly H1 neuron.
