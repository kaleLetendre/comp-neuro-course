F1 learning outcomes. What the listener should be able to do afterwards.

This document tells the generator what the lesson is for. Build toward these capabilities.

One. Compute a spike count rate from a raw spike train, and say what additional data would be needed to estimate a time dependent rate at one particular instant.

Two. Use the Fano factor as a diagnostic. Know what value a completely random Poisson process gives, and reason about what a departure in either direction implies about the underlying spiking.

Three. Choose a smoothing window and defend the choice, saying what a wide window costs and what a narrow one costs, and why no choice escapes both.

Four. Read a tuning curve, saying what its peak and its width each mean in practice.

Five. State the rate versus timing question as genuinely open, with one concrete piece of evidence on each side.

An important constraint for generation. These outcomes are also the questions the learner will be assessed on. Do not answer them directly and do not present them as a quiz within the audio or video. Teach the mechanisms they depend on, so that a listener who has understood the material can construct the answers. Explaining what the Fano factor measures, and why a refractory period imposes regularity, is correct. Announcing that the answer is therefore below one is not.
